YumShots Privacy Policy

Developer: PinPixels · Last updated: November 20, 2026

YumShots ("the app") is a food photography app made by PinPixels. This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and how you can remove your data. YumShots has no user accounts, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

Permissions and what they are used for

  • Camera — so you can shoot your food. Photos are taken and processed on your device.
  • Location (optional) — only if you turn on Find the place. You choose precise or approximate location. It is used only to look up nearby places for your photo.
  • Internet — used for the nearby-place search when Find the place is on. Your location is sent only for that search and is not stored by us.
  • Importing photos — photos you import come through the Android system photo picker, so the app only sees the photos you choose.
  • Saving photos — saved photos are written through Android's media store.

YumShots does not request storage or contacts permissions.

Data stored on your device

The working photo, saved edits, Memories and your looks are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us.

Deleting your data

To remove the working photo, saved edits, Memories and your looks, clear the app's storage (Settings → Apps → YumShots → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the app. Photos you saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them yourself.

Children

YumShots is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
